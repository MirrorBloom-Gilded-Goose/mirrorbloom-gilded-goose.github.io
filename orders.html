Orders & Institutions

The people who guard the world, hunt in it, teach it, and build it.

The Knights of Hope

The All Mother's personal knights protect her realm and Mirrorbloom, and exist to bridge the gap between mortals and her.

Almost every one of them is a famed warrior who died, and who showed such devotion and strength in life that she chose them herself. Almost: the living are not barred, and over the centuries a handful have been raised into the order while still breathing. The Mages College has never worked out what she is looking for when she does it.

They are led by Captain Reagan, who in life founded the first empire, Yenfin. He was so powerful that many believed he was an anomaly, which was later confirmed to be untrue. He now leads the order against threats across the realms, and when the All Mother is away, he guides lost souls to the afterlife himself.

The Marauders

Despite the name, the Marauders help people.

They are an institution spanning the whole continent, and they hunt cultists: those who worship the dark Fey Lords and Mistress Despair, and who commit as many atrocities as they can in the hope that Despair will overthrow the All Mother and bring on a dark age. The Marauders cull them by working with the empires and kingdoms, sharing intelligence and manpower across borders.

They were founded centuries ago in the Yenfin Empire. Lately they have had a great deal of work: Despair cults are rising across Solance at levels nobody alive has seen.

The ritual hunters

Everyone knows that injecting magic into the Mana Field by ritual is illegal. Fewer people know that there is said to be a single organization, crossing every border, that tracks down illegal ritual casters and kills them. That it exists is public knowledge. Nothing else about it is.

The Mages College

The great academy of the Shining Everlast, and the place most people mean when they say someone was properly trained. Professor Anna Linfay taught there before she left for the Gilded Goose, and recordings of her classes still circulate, which is how much of what this record knows about the wider world was first set down.

The College is proud, prestigious and not as free as it likes to think. Speculation about Umbrasol is not welcome in its classrooms; at least one lecturer has had to retract it on the record. Its students also have a habit of looking down on the Foundry, which is a mistake.

Somewhere in its portrait hall hangs Anna Linfay's portrait. It keeps ending up turned to face the wall.

The Foundry

The Foundry makes Hopelight, the fuel of the Everlast. It also makes almost everything else: tools, entire city blocks, and the advanced magical power grids the city runs on. Without it, the Everlast grinds to a halt within days, which makes it one of the most powerful factions in the empire whether the Senate likes it or not.

It is run by Rog, an old orc with a scraggly beard and brown coveralls, and an old friend of the Gilded Goose. People who cannot cast or fly can still put up a fight, and Foundry workers are nastier than anyone expects when they are angry.

The Dragon Layer

The Everlast's bard academy. The name is not a misspelling.

It was founded five years after the Great Umbrasol Siege by a bard named John Fevor, whose life's mission was not anything grand: he simply wanted to lie with a dragon. He succeeded, during the Siege, and made allies of the dragon and his kin along the way. Yes, his. Fevor had also hoped for half-dragon children, which scholars have noted is ironic. The alliance turned the tide on several fronts, with his dragons driving the enemy back alongside the other heroes of the War, and afterward he founded the academy.

He later moved away to live with his husband, the dragon Valek, and the two have adopted several dragons together.
