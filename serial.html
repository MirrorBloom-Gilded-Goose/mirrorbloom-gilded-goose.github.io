Tales of the Gilded Goose

A character-driven fantasy about found family, mirror worlds, and the fine art of causing exactly the right disaster.

The Gilded Goose finds things. Understands things. Breaks every rule that says they can't.

Crockett has run the guild for three hundred years, which is longer than most empires last and roughly three hundred years longer than anyone thought a goblin with his impulse control would survive. Under his watch, the Gilded Goose has become the Shining Everlast's most brilliant guild and its most expensive headache — advancing science, toppling monsters, and once turning half a city block into gold by accident. They always clean up their messes. Eventually.

Their latest job should be simple: dig into the ancient ruins beneath the Yenfin Empire, find what's down there, write it up over drinks.

But the ruins are older than they should be. The ground is thinner than it looks. And far beneath Solance, in the dark mirror of everything above, something has been waiting a very long time for the world to come to it.

Getting in was an accident. Getting out will cost them everything.
