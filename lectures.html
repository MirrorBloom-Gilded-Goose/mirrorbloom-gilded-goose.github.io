Linfay's Lecture Hall

The recorded classes of Professor Anna Linfay, as they open each chapter of the serial.

Before she ran research for the Gilded Goose, Anna Linfay taught at the Mages College, and her classes were recorded. From Chapter 2 on, every chapter of Tales of the Gilded Goose opens with a piece of one. They come in the order the chapters do, not the order of a course, so the subject jumps around the way a curious class would.

Reading along? and recordings from chapters you haven't reached stay hidden.
