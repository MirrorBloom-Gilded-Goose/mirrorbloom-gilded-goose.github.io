Crockett

Founder and guildmaster of the Gilded Goose, and the oldest goblin in the Everlast

Role
Founder and guildmaster
People
Goblin, pure and simple
Magic
Chaotic time magic
Challenge
25

Appearance

A small, very old goblin who wears his years the way a well-used tool wears its scratches: not diminished, just thoroughly proven. Bright-eyed, quick-fingered, and dressed like someone who long ago decided that dignity was a tax he refused to pay.

Personality

Chaotic in the way of something that has always been chaotic and has had centuries to refine the craft. His curiosity is not a habit; it is a force of nature with a budget line. He takes in outcasts, oddballs and anyone brilliant enough that no one else would have them, and he would burn the guildhall down before letting harm come to any of them. He would also, to be fair, possibly burn the guildhall down by accident.

Long ago the giants had a name for him: the goblin with a thousand questions.

Where he came from

Crockett is not from Mirrorbloom. He arrived through a doorway about two hundred and fifty years ago, after a long and difficult chapter of his life in another realm came to an end. Unlike the people born here, he is a realm walker, and he can leave. Where his long life comes from is a question he answers differently every time he is asked, and no two answers have ever agreed.

He fought in the Great Umbrasol Siege a hundred and twenty years ago, which is why he has friends, debts and history in the Everlast, Kelvarion, Yenfin and Vaelgard all at once.

Magic

Chaotic time magic: wilder in his youth, more controlled now, but fundamentally and permanently chaotic at its core. Age has taught him aim, not restraint.

Vesper Nightfall

His great rival is Vesper Nightfall, a vampire Grand Sorcerer from another realm with a rock-and-roll flair, and his wife and assistant, Isolde. Their contest in the frozen wastelands to the north began friendly and contained, and spiraled out of control over two days. The wastelands are a desert now. Monsters made of blood roam it, and time anomalies are scattered across it. It is rumored that the Archfey stepped in to stop the pair, and have watched both men ever since.

In the serial

Crockett narrates much of Tales of the Gilded Goose, leading the expedition to the ruins beneath the Yenfin Empire, the routine dig that turns out to be anything but. Centuries of chaos have taught him nearly everything except the lesson the journey below seems determined to teach: the difference between chaos for its own sake, and chaos with intention.
