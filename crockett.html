The Gilded Goose

Crockett

Founder & Guildmaster — The Oldest Goblin in the Everlast

Small humanoid (goblin), chaotic neutral — Guildmaster, CR 25 (75,000 XP)

Appearance

A small, very old goblin who wears his three hundred years the way a well-used tool wears its scratches — not diminished, just thoroughly proven. Bright-eyed, quick-fingered, and dressed like someone who long ago decided that dignity was a tax he refused to pay.

Personality

Chaotic in the way of something that has always been chaotic and has simply had three centuries to refine the craft. His curiosity is not a habit; it is a force of nature with a budget line. He takes in outcasts, oddballs, and anyone brilliant enough that no one else would have them, and he would burn the guildhall down before letting harm come to any of them. He would also, to be fair, possibly burn the guildhall down by accident.

Magic

Chaotic time magic — wilder in his youth, more controlled now, but fundamentally and permanently chaotic at its core. Age has taught him aim, not restraint. Where the magic came from, like the longevity itself, is a question Crockett answers differently every time he is asked, and no two answers have ever agreed.

In the Serial

Crockett narrates much of Tales of the Gilded Goose, leading the expedition to the ruins beneath the Yenfin Empire — the routine dig that turns out to be anything but. Three hundred years of chaos have taught him nearly everything except the one lesson the journey below seems determined to teach: the difference between chaos for its own sake, and chaos with intention.
