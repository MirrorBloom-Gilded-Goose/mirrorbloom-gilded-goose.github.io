Who's your reflection?

Everyone born in MirrorBloom is shackled to a mirror self in Umbrasol, whose heart runs opposite to their own. Answer five questions and meet yours.

1What do people call you?
2Which Archfey do you pray to?
3What are you proudest of in yourself?
4How will you leave your mark?
5Where do you live?

Your reflection doesn't know you exist. Probably. Read about the Law of Reflection and the Shackle, or meet the people living with theirs in Tales of the Gilded Goose.
