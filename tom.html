Tom

The quiet one of the Elite Four

Role
Elite Four
Craft
Spy, infiltrator, quiet knife

Appearance

Completely, deliberately, professionally unremarkable. Black hair, medium build, leather clothes that would pass in any market in any empire. Witnesses reliably remember someone standing where Tom stood. They just never remember him.

Personality

Speaks when he has something to say and is quiet otherwise: not cold, but controlled in a way that suggests the control came first and the calm was learned later. The guild is the closest thing to a home he acknowledges, and the Elite Four are the only people who can reliably pick him out of a crowd. That may be the highest trust he knows how to give.

Craft

The finest spy in the Shining Everlast, a title nobody can verify, because verifying it would require noticing him. Infiltration, extraction, and the kind of quiet knife-work the guild's official ledgers describe, in Sandy's tidy handwriting, as "consulting."

In the serial

Tom walks point on the expedition below: the first through every door, the last to be surprised. Where he came from and what made him is a story he has never told anyone, and the guild has learned not to ask. The story below the world has a way of asking questions no one wants asked.
