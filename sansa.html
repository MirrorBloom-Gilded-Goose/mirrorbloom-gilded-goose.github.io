The Gilded Goose

Sansa

The Iron Shield of the Elite Four

Large humanoid (half-giant), chaotic good — Barbarian 13, CR 11 (7,200 XP)

Appearance

Very tall — genuinely Large by the standards of the creature type table — with blonde braids, an easy grin, and the proportions of someone who once carried a wagon home because the horse looked tired.

Personality

Direct, warm, and possessed of a specific kind of honor that is neither naive nor inflexible. The best drinking company in the guild and the worst person to face across a battlefield. She is also a princess, a fact she mentions never, thinks about constantly, and left home specifically to stop being.

Craft

Front-line devastation. Sansa is the wall the guild stands behind and the hammer it swings — the Iron Shield in both senses. Her rage is not loss of control; it is the one place where all the things she refuses to carry get put down at once.

In the Serial

Sansa holds the line on the expedition below, cheerfully volunteering for every duty except the one waiting for her back home. Her story keeps circling the same question from new directions: the difference between the responsibility you are handed, and the responsibility you choose.
