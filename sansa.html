Sansa

The iron shield of the Elite Four, and a princess of Vaelgard

Role
Elite Four
From
Vaelgard
Craft
Front-line fighter, ice and lightning

Appearance

Tall, blonde-braided, with an easy grin and a giant axe. At rest she looks like what she is, a tall warrior in fighting shape. When she gets serious, her mutation wakes and she visibly bulks up in real time, which is usually the last warning anyone gets. She channels ice and lightning natively.

Personality

Direct, warm, and possessed of a specific kind of honor that is neither naive nor inflexible. The best drinking company in the guild and the worst person to face across a battlefield. She is also a princess, a fact she mentions never, thinks about constantly, and left home specifically to stop being.

Home

Sansa comes from Vaelgard, where everyone trains as a warrior first and a tradesperson second, a custom her great-grandfather began when he broke a siege during the War. Its general, Ragnar, is retiring, and her mother wants Sansa home to take command of the army. Her mother always favored her brothers.

In the serial

Sansa holds the line on the expedition below, cheerfully volunteering for every duty except the one waiting for her back home. Her story keeps circling the same question from new directions: the difference between the responsibility you are handed, and the responsibility you choose.
