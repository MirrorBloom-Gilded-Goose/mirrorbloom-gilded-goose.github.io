The Gilded Goose

Will

The Physician of the Elite Four

Medium humanoid (human), chaotic neutral — Cleric 10 / Wizard 6 (Biomancy), CR 13 (10,000 XP)

Appearance

A lanky man with pure white hair and the distracted energy of someone whose mind is always three steps ahead of the conversation. His eyes are arcane lenses — his own design — that whir faintly when he focuses. Around his neck, never removed, hangs an amulet of the All Mother.

Personality

A mad scientist who happens to be a doctor, or a doctor who happens to be a mad scientist, depending on which of his experiments is currently on fire. He stores his own alchemical compounds in glands he grew for the purpose, which tells you most of what you need to know about his relationship with the phrase “acceptable risk.”

Craft

Biomancy — the meeting point of divine healing and arcane experimentation. Will is both a faithful cleric of the All Mother and a scientist who takes the body apart to see how the miracle works. The tension between those two things is not lost on him. It is, in fact, the axis his whole life turns on.

In the Serial

Will keeps the expedition alive, patched together, and occasionally mutated slightly for their own good. The journey below Solance keeps handing him cases where faith and science give different answers — and one of these days, he is going to have to decide which one he trusts when they disagree.
