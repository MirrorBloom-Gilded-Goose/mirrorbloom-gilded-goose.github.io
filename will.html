Will

The physician of the Elite Four

Role
Elite Four
Craft
Cleric and biomancer
Faith
The All Mother

Appearance

A lanky man with pure white hair and the distracted energy of someone whose mind is always three steps ahead of the conversation. His eyes are arcane lenses of his own design that whir faintly when he focuses. Around his neck, never removed, hangs an amulet of the All Mother.

Personality

A mad scientist who happens to be a doctor, or a doctor who happens to be a mad scientist, depending on which experiment is currently on fire. He stores his own alchemical compounds in glands he grew for the purpose, which tells you most of what you need to know about his relationship with the phrase "acceptable risk."

Craft

Biomancy: the meeting point of divine healing and arcane experiment. Will heals by stabbing a patient with a fingernail. His magic glows green, and when he uses his own blood to carry it, it mists out red shot through with green, the cost visible in the same breath as the cure. He is both a faithful cleric of the All Mother and a scientist who takes the body apart to see how the miracle works, and the tension between those two things is the axis his whole life turns on.

In the serial

Will keeps the expedition alive, patched together, and occasionally mutated slightly for its own good. The journey below keeps handing him cases where faith and science give different answers, and one of these days he will have to decide which one he trusts when they disagree.
