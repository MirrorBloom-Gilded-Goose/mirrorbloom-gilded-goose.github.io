The Gilded Goose

Cherry

The Wild Card of the Elite Four

Medium fiend (tiefling), chaotic good — Sorcerer 15 (Wild Magic), CR 12 (8,400 XP)

Appearance

Paper-white skin, heavily scarred. Bright red hair, black horns, and black tears that fall whether she is crying or not. Her mouth opens a little too wide when she smiles, and her claws are sharper than any tiefling's should be. She dresses in a white corset and skirt, immaculate against everything the rest of her appearance suggests.

Her appearance is the first thing people notice, and frequently the last thing they mention twice.

Personality

Loving, chaotic, and completely sincere about both. She has a genuine mean streak — ask the last person who stared too long — and a heart that is bigger than the guild's annual damage bill. She views Crockett as an uncle in the sense that matters: the one who took her in when nobody else would open the door.

Magic

Wild magic with a circus running through every spell: calliope music from nowhere, confetti that falls upward, phantom acrobats tumbling through the air where a fireball should be, and the smell of burnt sugar hanging over the aftermath. It is beautiful, it is unsettling, and it is never, ever quiet.

In the Serial

Cherry is one of the Elite Four accompanying Crockett on the Yenfin expedition. She is ashamed of what she looks like and refuses to let it stop her — and somewhere on the road below, the story keeps asking her what she intends to be: something that frightens people, or something that stands between them and the things that should.
