Cherry

The wild card of the Elite Four

Role
Elite Four
Magic
Wild magic with a carnival in it
Height
Four foot eleven

Appearance

Cherry was not born the way she looks. She is human, transformed: black horns, paper-white skin heavily scarred, black eyes that weep black tears whether she is crying or not, bright red hair, and a mouth that stretches far too wide when she smiles. She is four foot eleven. In public she is always masked, and she is deeply ashamed of the face underneath.

Personality

Loving, chaotic, and completely sincere about both. She has a genuine mean streak, ask the last person who stared too long, and a mischievous one too, and a heart bigger than the guild's annual damage bill. She views Crockett as an uncle in the sense that matters: the one who took her in when nobody else would open the door.

Magic

When Cherry casts, the air changes color and carnival music plays from nowhere. She dissolves into pink orbs to cross a room. She spins bubblegum webbing, turns her scream into a sonic shockwave through a mana megaphone, and fills a fight with lifelike balloon decoys. Her wild surges have been known to leave balloons behind that turn out to be landmines. Her most dangerous trick, an illusion exhaled as a red cloud through that unhinged jaw, means taking off the mask.

In the serial

Cherry is one of the Elite Four on the Yenfin expedition. She is ashamed of what she looks like and refuses to let it stop her, and somewhere on the road below, the story keeps asking her what she intends to be: something that frightens people, or something that stands between them and the things that should.
