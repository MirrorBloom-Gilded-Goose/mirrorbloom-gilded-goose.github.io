Rotblossom Crawler

Small plant, neutral evil — Challenge 3 (700 XP) — serves The Bloated Lady

Bloated, fruiting bodies that detach from the Bloated Lady's own form and scuttle off to spread her garden further. Each one is hungry in exactly the way she is.
