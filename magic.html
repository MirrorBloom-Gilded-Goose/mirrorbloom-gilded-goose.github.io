The Systems of the World

Magic & the Law of Reflection

How power works in Mirrorbloom — and what every use of it costs.

The Law of Reflection

Intent, Not Act

The Law of Reflection does not read deeds. It reads intent. A kindness performed for show moves nothing; a genuine change of heart moves the whole scale. When a person in one half of the world truly changes — grows kinder, grows crueler, grows cold, grows brave — that change mirrors across to their counterpart in the other half, inverted, whether either of them wants it or not.

This is why the wise of both halves are careful with their hearts. Goodness generated above feeds something hungry below. Cruelty below brightens something above. The ledger always balances, and no one has ever found the accountant.

The Shackle System

Every person born in Mirrorbloom is bound at birth to a mirror self — a counterpart in the opposite half whose alignment runs inverse to their own. Scholars call the binding the Shackle. It cannot be seen, weighed, or cut, and it does not care about distance.

Most people never meet their mirror self and live no worse for it. But the Shackle is always pulling: when one half of a pair changes in intent, the other half shifts to keep the balance. No Shackle has ever been broken. What would happen if one were is a question the courts of both halves consider too dangerous to test — which has never once stopped anyone from wondering.

The Three Layers of Magic

1 — The Personal Core

Every living thing carries a mana core: a personal reservoir of magic, small in most, vast in a few. A core is shaped by temperament as much as training — magic takes the color of the soul that holds it. Crockett's core runs to chaos and time; Cherry's erupts in circus light and calliope sound; Will's bends toward flesh and mending. The core is why no two casters' magic ever looks quite the same, even casting the same spell.

2 — The Ambient Field

Mana saturates the world itself — an ocean both halves swim in. Casters draw on the ambient field to fuel workings their core alone could never sustain, the way a mill draws on a river. The field is thicker in some places than others: dense over ley line convergences, thin over old battlefields, and strange beyond description near doorways.

3 — Field Influence

The rarest tier. Beings of sufficient power do not merely draw on the field — they inject their own magic into it, bending ambient reality around themselves. Weather that obeys a mood. Ground that remembers a grudge. A room that is larger inside than out. The gods and the Archfey live permanently at this tier; a handful of mortals have touched it. Most regretted it.

Applied Magic of the Everlast

Hopelight

The Shining Everlast runs on Hopelight: refined magic drawn from a dense convergence of ley lines beneath the capital. It lights the towers, drives the monorails, and powers the empire's industry — ambient field magic harnessed at civilizational scale. The Hopelight Institution, the empire's greatest academy, exists to study, refine, and jealously regulate it. The Gilded Goose's Head of Research taught there, once, before she found somewhere more interesting to be.

Doorways & Crossings

The fourteen divine realms connect to Mirrorbloom through doorways. Some are fixed and well-mapped, guarded or worshipped accordingly. Others wander, opening in places that held nothing the morning before. Crossing between Solance and Umbrasol themselves is another matter entirely — the halves were not meant to touch, and the places where the boundary runs thin are the most dangerous ground in the world. Ancient ruins have a way of sitting on exactly that kind of ground.
