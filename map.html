The Map

Solance from above, all the way to the edge. Cross the mirror and the map turns over with you.

Show

The rim of the world is the Boundary. The oceans pour off the edge into open space, the ruins of failed colonies drift beyond it, and Umbrasol glows somewhere underneath.

A sketch, not a survey. Coastlines and pin positions follow the record (mountains in Solmarch's north, desert in its south, Kenanism out on the far eastern reach) but they will change as the world is drawn properly.

Read about the routes in Roads, Skies & Tunnels, and the nations in The Thirteen Nations.
