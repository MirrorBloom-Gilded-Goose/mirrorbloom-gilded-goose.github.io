The Gilded Goose

Part research guild, part mercenary outfit, part organized disaster.

“Find it. Understand it. Share it. Break the rules that say you can't.”

The Guild

One of the top five active guilds in the Shining Everlast. The public loves them — the free meals, the spectacles, the way they always pay for what they break. The Senate is split fifty-fifty on whether they are the empire's greatest asset or its most expensive liability, but every senator on both sides of that line acknowledges their value. The Gilded Goose causes catastrophic, costly disasters with remarkable regularity. They have also, without exception, cleaned up every one.

The guildhall stands in the heart of the capital and is significantly larger on the inside than the outside — a fact the guild has never explained and the city has stopped asking about.

Three Divisions

The ScrollResearchThe guild's founding purpose: find it, understand it, share it. A research institution that learned early that research doesn't fund itself.
The SwordMercenary workResearch requires travel, travel requires protection, and protection — done well — pays for everything else.

The Council

G
Gerald Swordmaster An older, drunken gentleman with a heart of gold. A fool by reputation and something close to it by choice — being underestimated is a weapon, and in actual combat he is a menace.
S
Sandy Head of Administration Small, blonde, green-eyed, and genuinely gentle. Not soft — there is a backbone in Sandy that becomes visible the moment someone threatens her ledgers or her people.
