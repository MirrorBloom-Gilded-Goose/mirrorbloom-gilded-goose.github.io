The Gilded Goose

Part research guild, part mercenary outfit, part organized disaster.

Find it. Understand it. Share it. Break the rules that say you can't.The guild's motto

The guild

One of the top five active guilds in the Shining Everlast. The public loves them: the free meals, the spectacles, the way they always pay for what they break. The Senate is split fifty-fifty on whether they are the empire's greatest asset or its most expensive liability, but every senator on both sides acknowledges their value. The Gilded Goose causes catastrophic, costly disasters with remarkable regularity. It has also, without exception, cleaned up every one.

The guildhall stands in the heart of the capital and is significantly larger on the inside than the outside, a fact the guild has never explained and the city has stopped asking about.

Three divisions

The Scroll
Research, the guild's founding purpose: find it, understand it, share it. It learned early that research does not fund itself.
The Sword
Mercenary work. Research requires travel, travel requires protection, and protection done well pays for everything else.
Administration
The quiet miracle that keeps the other two solvent, legal, and out of Senate hearings. Mostly.

The guildmaster

C

Crockett

Founder and guildmaster

A small, very old goblin with chaotic time magic, catastrophic curiosity, and the most expensive line of credit in the empire.

The council

A

Anna Linfay

Head of Research

Stern, precise, black hair and glasses, and she can fly. A former professor at the Mages College, whose recorded lectures still circulate. She is the one who keeps Crockett in check.

G

Gerald

Swordmaster

An older, drunken gentleman with a heart of gold, constantly underestimated and a menace in actual combat. He believes freedom has a weight, and that the weight is accountability, not control.

S

Sandy

Head of Administration

Small, blonde, green-eyed and genuinely gentle. Not soft: there is a backbone in Sandy that shows the moment someone threatens her ledgers or her people.

The Elite Four

The guild's sharpest edge: its most powerful members in combat and capability, and the heart of its strangest family.
