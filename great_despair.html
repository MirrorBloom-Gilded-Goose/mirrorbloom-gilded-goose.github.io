The Great Despair

The Uncrowned Queen

Aspect
The god below
Twin
The Great Hope, She Who Does Not Know
Realm
The Hollow Court
Nature
Gargantuan fey, lawful evil
Challenge
30

She remembers the Breaking, or believes she does, which amounts to the same agony either way. In her telling — and it is the only telling the Hollow Court is permitted to repeat — a crown was taken from her in the moment of the world's splitting, a right, a throne, something owed that was stripped away by forces she can no longer name. She has spent every year since trying to reclaim it. There is no crown. There was never a crown. What she actually lost was the Great Hope herself, and somewhere beneath the many eyes and the jagged smile, a part of her may even understand this — which is precisely why the lie has to be so carefully, so violently maintained. She wears a twisted mockery of regalia, dresses for a war she's still fighting, and rules the Hollow Court not as a monarch enthroned but as a general still marching toward a victory that was never the actual goal.

Mistress Despair

Below the glass she is Mistress Despair, and most of Umbrasol worships her. The two red moons that hang over Umbrasol are her eyes, and they are always open.

Her face is not beautiful by any standard Solance would recognize: many eyes, and teeth that rise jagged and sharp. The body beneath it is another matter, and the pairing is deliberately unsettling. In Umbrasol it reads as holy. The closer a face comes to hers, the more devout and the more desirable its owner is taken to be, which is why Umbrasol's nobility finds monstrous features beautiful and means it sincerely.

Her church teaches of an afterlife called the Gorge, where the essence of the damned is collected to fill her cup and the cups of her guests. Above, cults in her name are rising across Solance at levels not seen in living memory, and the Marauders hunt them.
