The surface

What everyone knows

Every child above and below.

  • The two goddesses exist, the Great Hope and the Great Despair.
  • Solance and Umbrasol mirror each other, and Umbrasol is the evil twin.
  • The Law of Reflection, as folklore: kindness above is paid for below.
  • The races, and that Kenanism is where most of them came from.
  • The Archfey are gods, and you pray to them.

What nobody at the surface knows is how any of it actually works.

Just below the surface

What the educated know

Anyone trained at a mages' college, or with long experience of the world.

  • The Mana Field exists, and it reacts to what is done in it.
  • Rituals are illegal.

They do not know the real reason rituals are illegal, or how dangerous they truly are. Most of them never ask.
