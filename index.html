A homebrew world for D&D 5e & the Tales of the Gilded Goose serial

Solance — MIRRORBLOOM — Umbrasol

One flat world, split top and bottom like a reflection on still water. Everything beautiful above feeds something hungry below — and everyone born here is shackled to the self on the other side of the glass.

☉ Cosmology The Law of Reflection, the Breaking, the two gods, and the Archfey courts. ✦ Timeline Five eras of history, from the Primordial round world to the Fourth Age. ♜ The Everlast The vertical empire of Hopelight — and the Last Shine, its mirror below. 🪿 Gilded Goose The guild, the council, the Elite Four, and their catastrophic guildmaster. 📚 Library The serial, the bestiary, adventures, and DM tools.

The Web Serial — New chapters Mon / Wed / Fri

Tales of the Gilded Goose

Crockett has run the guild for three hundred years. Their latest job should be simple: dig into the ruins beneath the Yenfin Empire, find what's down there, write it up over drinks. Getting in was an accident. Getting out will cost them everything.

The Law of Reflection: beauty and goodness generated above directly feeds horror and evil below. It is not metaphorical, and it is not optional. A kindness performed in Mirrorbloom is, somewhere beneath it, paid for.
