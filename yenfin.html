The Yenfin Empire

The Yenfin Empire

The first empire, still living in the light of its best years.

Yenfin is a conservative, traditionalist monarchy, and by most accounts the oldest empire in Mirrorbloom. It was founded by Captain Reagan, who now leads the Knights of Hope. Whether Yenfin truly came first is still argued: some insist the elves founded the Shining Everlast before it.

The golden age

A hundred and twenty years ago, during the Great Umbrasol Siege, Yenfin was a true power and one of the heroes of the War. Its armies stood among the great key points that broke the invasion, and the years that followed were a genuine golden age.

The Marauders, who now hunt Despair cultists across the continent, were founded in Yenfin centuries ago.

The fade

Yenfin never stopped looking back at that age, and that is its problem. Fixated on past glory, it has slowly faded. Other nations respect it less than they used to. Its people are leaving, emigrating to places that are looking forward instead.

Its ground is also unsettled. The ancient ruins beneath Yenfin are where the Gilded Goose's expedition began, and the land around the dig is not as stable as it should be.

The boy emperor

Yenfin's current emperor is fifteen and only recently inherited the throne. His name is not recorded here. He is a snob, as might be expected of a boy raised to believe his empire was the greatest in the world, but he genuinely wants to do good, and he badly needs someone to teach him how.
